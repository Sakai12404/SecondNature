FormForge

FormForge is a 10-week class that covers basic Computer-Aided Design (CAD) in Onshape and related topics, such as using a 3D printer and dimensioning. This course will prepare students for more advanced CAD in robotics, manufacturing, engineering, and many other related careers/activities.
